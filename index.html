Questions?

Do I need a gym?

No. Programs are built around where you'll actually train: a gym, at home, outdoors or a mix.

I haven't exercised in years. Is this for me?

Yes, that's exactly who it's for. We start where you are and build up from there.

What if I have an injury or health condition?

Tell us on the form. Before you start, everyone completes the Australian standard pre-exercise health screen, and if you need the OK from your GP or physio first, Jorge will talk you through it.

How does online coaching work?

You get your program on your phone, send short videos of your lifts for feedback, check in each week, and have a 1-on-1 catch-up each month.

What does it cost?

$49 per week plus a one-off $35 sign-up fee. There's nothing to pay to fill in the form or have the first chat.

What happens after 8 weeks?

We re-test your goals so you can see your progress, then you can keep going with a new block.
